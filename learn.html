Learn the Basics of Credit Cards


Welcome to Credit Cards 101!


What is a credit card?

A credit card allows you to borrow money from the card issuer to make purchases or cash advances. Think of it as borrowing a book from a library. You can use the book, but you have to return it by a certain date. Similarly, with a credit card, you can use the borrowed money, but you have to pay it back within a specified time frame.



Benefits of using a credit card

  • Protection: Credit cards allow you to be protected from fraud and unauthorized charges
  • Points & Rewards: They also allow you to earn points and rewards from everyday purchases that can be used towards travel, gift cards, and services.
  • Special Offers: Various credit cards offer exclusive offers and services such as access to airport lounges, extended warranty on purchases, and covered subscriptions on streaming platforms.
  • Build Credit: Through a good payment history, credit cards can help you create a strong portfolio for future financial success.


How to use a credit card responsibly

  • Pay your balance in full each month to avoid interest charges.
  • Keep your credit utilization low (ideally below 30% of your credit limit).
  • Make payments on time to maintain a good credit score.
  • Monitor your statements for any unauthorized transactions.


Myth or Fact? #1

Credit cards are only for people with perfect credit scores.

Answer: Myth!

You don't need a perfect credit score to get a credit card. There are many cards designed for people with average, fair, or even building credit (like secured cards).



Myth or Fact? #2

A high credit limit is a good thing.

Answer: Fact!

If you manage your credit cards wisely, a high credit limit can be an advantage. Thirty percent of your credit score is based on your debt-to-credit ratio (the amount you owe in proportion to your total credit limit). If you have a high credit limit and you keep your balances low, your debt-to-credit ratio is also low, which can help your credit score.



Myth or Fact? #3

You may be able to upgrade or downgrade your card without a new inquiry or application.

Answer: Fact!

Yes, this is called a "Product Change," and you can often upgrade or downgrade your credit card without a new credit inquiry, especially if you are a loyal customer or if the change is made within a short period of time. Although it depends on the credit card provider, it does not hurt to contact them.
